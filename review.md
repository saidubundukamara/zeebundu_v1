About Us

Website copy heading  A growing group with a practical purpose

ZEEBUNDU GROUP OF COMPANIES is a diversified business group with operations and projects in Sierra Leone. We bring together consumer services, production, trade, agriculture and property development under a common business vision.

Our approach is to build businesses that support one another while meeting distinct customer needs. Retail and food services complement our fuel operations. Agriculture connects with our food production ambitions. Construction and supply activities support the facilities through which our businesses grow.

As we develop our portfolio, our focus remains practical: reliable service, responsible management, useful products and opportunities for people to contribute through their work.

Mission

To develop and manage businesses that deliver useful products and dependable services, create employment and contribute to economic opportunity in Sierra Leone.

Vision

To become a trusted Sierra Leonean business group whose connected enterprises support stronger communities and lasting economic growth.

Our values

• Integrity — We expect honest dealings and responsible care of company and customer resources.
• Accountability — We set clear responsibilities and expect people to take ownership of their work.
• Service — We listen to customers and work to meet their needs with respect.
• Quality and safety — We seek consistent standards in our products, workplaces and services.
• Enterprise — We invest in practical ideas that can become sustainable businesses.
• Community — We value employment, local relationships and the communities in which we work.
What connects our businesses

Each business has its own customers and operating requirements. Across the group, we work toward common standards for customer care, staff development, maintenance, recordkeeping and financial responsibility.
