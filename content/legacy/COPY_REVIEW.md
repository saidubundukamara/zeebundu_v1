## 1. How to use this document

This document contains the proposed text for the new website: the group pages, the 8 sectors and all 16 businesses. Once you approve it, we load it straight into the website's content system (CMS). After launch your team can edit any of it themselves.

Where the text came from:

- The old website had some text for about half of the businesses. Most of it was generic template text written for the United States (US phone numbers, prices in dollars, US certifications, hotels in the Swiss Alps and the Maldives). None of it described Zeebundu's real businesses.
- We kept what was useful, rewrote it for Sierra Leone, and removed everything that was invented or foreign.
- For the 6 businesses that had no text at all, we drafted short, modest copy for you to correct.
- We have **not** made up any numbers, dates, locations, prices, licences or partner names. Wherever a real fact is needed, you will see a placeholder.

### How to review

1. Read each section. You can **edit the text directly** in this document or **add a comment** beside it.
2. At the end of each section there is a review line. Mark it:
   - ✅ **Approved**: use as written
   - ✏️ **Changes**: we have edited it or left comments
3. Fill in as many **[TODO: client]** items as you can. Anything left blank will be hidden on the website until you supply it; the page will still work.
4. Section 5 lists every open question in one place, if you prefer to answer them as a list.
