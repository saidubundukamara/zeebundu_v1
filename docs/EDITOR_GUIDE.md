# Zeebundu website — editor guide

One page for everyone who updates the website. The CMS lives at **zeebundu.com/admin**.

## Logging in

Go to `/admin` and sign in with the email and password you were given. Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password** on the login screen. Never share a login: ask an administrator for your own account.

## What you can edit

| Your role           | You can edit                                                                     |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Business editor** | Your business page, news tagged to your business, your images and your enquiries |
| **Group editor**    | Every business, all news, impact programmes, leadership, pages and the homepage  |
| **Super admin**     | Everything, plus user accounts and group contact details                         |

If something you need is missing from your menu, ask a super admin.

## Updating your business page

1. Open **Businesses** and click your business.
2. Work through the tabs:
   - **Overview:** tagline (one line), summary (1–2 sentences for cards and Google), logo, main photo and the overview text.
   - **Services & stats:** products and services, key numbers (only real, current figures), and gallery photos.
   - **Extras:** optional sections such as exchange rates, loan products, product sizes and prices, or rooms and amenities. Click **Add Extra section** and pick one.
   - **Locations & contact:** each branch with district, address, phone and opening hours. **"Enquiries go to"** is the email address that receives website messages. It is never shown on the site.
   - **SEO:** the title and description Google shows. Click **Auto-generate** if unsure.
3. Your changes save as a **draft** automatically. Nothing changes on the live site yet.
4. Click the **eye icon** (Live Preview) to see the page update as you type, on phone, tablet and desktop sizes.
5. When you're happy, click **Publish changes**. The live site updates straight away.

To schedule a change for later, use the arrow next to **Publish changes** and choose **Schedule publish**. Scheduled changes go live at the next daily publishing run, at 6am (Freetown time), so a change set for 2pm appears at 6am the following day. If it must go live at an exact time, publish it by hand at that time.

## News

1. **News & press → Create new.**
2. Add a title, a short excerpt (shown on cards), a main photo and the article text.
3. In the sidebar, choose the **category** (News, Press release or Story) and, if it's about one business, the **business**. Business editors must choose their own business.
4. Preview, then **Publish**. The date is set automatically when you first publish.

## Photos and files

- Upload images wide and sharp: at least **1600 px** wide for main photos. The site makes smaller versions automatically.
- **Alt text is required.** Describe what's in the picture for people using screen readers, for example "Staff filling water bottles at the Wellington factory". Don't write "image of…".
- After uploading, click the image and drag the **focal point** onto the most important part, so it isn't cropped out on phones.
- Only upload photos you have the right to use.

## Enquiries

Messages sent through the website appear under **Enquiries** and are emailed to your "Enquiries go to" address.

1. Reply to the customer by email or phone. The notification email's reply button goes straight to them.
2. Set the **Status** to _In progress_, then _Closed_. Mark junk as _Spam_.
3. Use **Internal notes** for anything colleagues should know. Customers never see these.

You can't change what the customer wrote. That's deliberate.

## Writing tips

- Write for customers in Sierra Leone: plain English, short sentences, prices in **Leones (Le)**.
- Always spell the name **Zeebundu**. Never "ZeeBundu" or "Bundu".
- Only publish facts you can stand behind: real numbers, licences you actually hold, current prices.
- Phone and WhatsApp numbers in international format: **+232 76 123 456**.

## If something goes wrong

- **Made a mistake?** Open **Versions** (top right of any page) and restore an earlier version.
- **Page looks wrong after publishing?** Refresh the page. If it's still wrong, contact the web team with the page address and a screenshot.
- **Locked out or need access to another business?** Ask a super admin.
